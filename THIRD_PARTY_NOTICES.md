## Locally hosted open fonts

The following unmodified fonts are bundled in `public/fonts/` under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Their original copyright and license texts are preserved in `public/fonts/licenses/`. Exact asset URLs and SHA-256 checksums are recorded in `public/fonts/sources.json`.

- Noto Sans SC and Noto Serif SC: https://github.com/google/fonts (official Google Fonts WOFF2 subsets, regular/bold/black).
- LXGW WenKai TC: https://github.com/lxgw/LxgwWenKaiTC (official Google Fonts WOFF2 subsets, regular).
- Smiley Sans (得意黑): https://github.com/atelier-anchor/smiley-sans (unmodified WOFF2 distributed by the pinned `font-smiley-sans@1.0.0` package; original author's OFL license retained).
- ZCOOL KuaiLe: https://github.com/googlefonts/zcool-kuaile (official Google Fonts TTF).

Fonts are served by the website itself; visitors do not need to install them or connect to Google Fonts. Template typography and density rules also draw on the Baoyu typography reference and the shared measurement/rendering approach in https://github.com/geekfoxcharlie/XHS-TextCard (MIT). No XHS-TextCard source code is included.
